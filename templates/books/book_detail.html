About this book

A retired lighthouse keeper discovers that the lamp she tended for forty years guides more than ships, and must decide who to let through before the next storm.

Forty years on the same rock have taught Ada Marlow patience, and very little about people. When a boat appears that is not on any chart, she opens the logbook her predecessor left behind and finds her own name in it, written decades before she arrived.

A quiet, atmospheric novel about duty, weather, and the kindness of strangers.
